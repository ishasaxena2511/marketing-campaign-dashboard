This shift is mathematically projected to lift blended portfolio ROI from **41.56% to ~60.06%**, expanding net profit by **~₹35L to ₹48L** with **zero increase in total media expenditure**.

## 3. Critical Analytical Findings & Operational Diagnostics

### A. Sunk Costs & Bidding Inefficiencies in Google Search
Google absorbs **43.5% of total media capital** (₹1.15Cr), yet delivers our second-lowest direct response ROI (28.61%). Our audit isolated **2 extreme spend anomalies (> ₹22.5L each)** resulting from unconstrained broad-match bidding and lack of campaign-level spend caps. Capping maximum campaign flight budgets at ₹2.5L and eliminating non-converting broad search terms will free **₹17.23L** in capital without harming core branded lead flow.

### B. The Meta Split: Instagram vs. Facebook Divergence
While Facebook and Instagram are managed within the same Ads Manager suite, their unit economics have diverged significantly:
- **Instagram:** Delivers **70.78% ROI** at an efficient CAC of **₹410.02**, driven by strong engagement on Reels and carousel product formats across the 21–35 demographic.
- **Facebook:** Stagnates at an **18.78% ROI** and an elevated CAC of **₹708.29** (+72.7% more expensive than Instagram). Audience saturation and declining click-to-lead throughput require shifting **25% of Facebook's spend (₹12.79L)** directly to Instagram.

### C. The Underleveraged Email Engine
Email is currently treated as an operational afterthought, receiving just **0.77% of total budget** (₹2.03L). However, it generated **205.95% ROI** with an exceptional **Customer Acquisition Cost of ₹17.36**. Retention, re-engagement, and abandoned-cart sequences can absorb significant additional volume before hitting diminishing returns.

### D. Full-Funnel Conversion Bottlenecks
Evaluating our aggregate funnel throughput reveals the following stage drop-offs:

- **Primary Leakage:** The largest single efficiency loss occurs at the **Click-to-Lead stage**, where **79.62% of traffic bounces**. Unifying landing page load speeds and testing progressive profiling forms can significantly expand downstream lead capture.

### E. Festive Seasonality Multiplier (India Market)
Analyzing performance across calendar quarters reveals that Q4 campaigns (coinciding with Dussehra, Diwali, and festive sales) generated an aggregate **2.92x ROAS**, compared to **1.24x ROAS** during baseline Q1–Q3 operating periods. This represents an empirical **+135.5% festive performance premium**.
